Klein bottles

Klein bottle. A ring whose tube turns half a turn through w as it goes around, so it comes back flipped. No inside, no outside, and in 4D it never passes through itself. It is a surface, so slices are curves. In Shadow, the bright line starts on top of the tube and returns underneath; it takes two laps to close.

3D Klein bottle (S² × S¹). The same idea one dimension up: a sphere carried around a loop that comes back mirrored, inside out. It can’t fit in 4D without passing through itself, just as the glass Klein bottle can’t in 3D. Its slices are surfaces: a level slice through its middle is exactly the glass bottle, and stepping ana pinches off the thin neck first. In Shadow, the bright lines are the glass bottle’s two outlines; after one lap each becomes the other.

Your frame basis in world x y z w

The compass in the corner of the view shows the world x, y, z and w axes from your camera. An arrow is shorter the more its axis leaves your slice, and dashed once it is mostly out of it. Axes too short to draw are listed under it: ⊥ means mostly out of your slice, · means pointing along your line of sight.
