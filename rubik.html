4D Rubik’s Cube

A 3×3×3×3 puzzle with 80 movable pieces. You stand in a 3D slice of it: you see the part of the puzzle your slice cuts, and a twist turns a whole 3×3×3 block of it. Step ana or kata to slide the slice along the fourth axis, or rotate it to cut the puzzle at a new angle.
