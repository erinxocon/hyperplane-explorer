# Cat (`src/cat/`)

The OIIA meme cat spinning in 4D before a 4D TV playing the song. Two walker entries, redrawn every frame. `slice4d.js`, `spins.js` and `beat.js` are pure and tested. `inView(V, C, M)` gives `G = Q·M` (rows R, U, −F, A) and the center in your coordinates.

- **TV**: a 3D shape times [−h, h] along its own w. `thickSlice(G, h, off)` gives a 3×3 map L, a shift and a slab of two clip planes. det L = 1/b: mirrored when b < 0, clamped small edge-on. Drawn with `clippingPlanes` and a BackSide copy for cut faces, swapping to reversed winding when mirrored.
- **Cat shape**: skin point s, normal n, `depth` r, turned θ into w: (s − r(1 − cos θ)·n, r sin θ), |θ| ≤ 90°, so a ball becomes a 4D ball.
- **JS cut** (`roundCutter`): 17 θ steps make each skin triangle and step a prism. A prism crossed only on its upright edges gives one triangle; others split into three tetrahedra and march. Upright crossings are exact roots of A + B cos θ + C sin θ; the rest are interpolated and refined. 4–15 ms, so a mesh is re-cut only when its G or offset changes.
- **GPU cut** (`shaders/cut.tsl.js`, real WebGPU only): compute passes append one number per crossed triangle (via `cutCases`, tested against the JS cutter), an indirect draw takes them, and the vertex shader finds each crossing, so nothing uploads per cut. Unwound, so double-sided with the normal unflipped. Drawn as 3 vertices, one instance per triangle, because Firefox silently skips an indirect draw past its vertex buffers. `window.cat4d.wgsl()` logs and returns its WGSL; its compute passes come from three's private `renderer._nodes`, so recheck after upgrading three.
- **Spins** (`SPINS`): rotations in the cat's own coordinates, all back at the start after three turns, each with `turns` and `best`. `window.cat4d.pose(spin, degrees)` is for tests.
- **Views** (math in `spins.js`): Best view (`BEST`) picks a slice holding the spin's planes, where it's a plain 3D turn; double rotations and Tumble have none, so Shadow with trails. Axis cross (`CROSS`) and film strip (`filmAngles`) replace the cut in Slice, hide the TV, and draw from the mesh pool (`cutAt`) shared with Stack.
- **Shadow**: three w layers are projected in the vertex shader, so a frame only sets `catShadow.G` and `.at`. Each vertex sums its triangles' 4D bivectors (`layerBivectors`), giving the projected normal from the map's Jacobian (`shadowVertex` is the same math for tests).
- **Model** (`model.js`): an SDF meshed by surface nets at load, ~18k vertices; slices and Shadow work per vertex, so keep it small. `tests/unit/cat-model.test.js` checks it stays closed. `fur.js` patches `MeshStandardMaterial`. The coat is a 4D pattern read at `catPos + catW·WDIR`, so stripes slide as you step ana; markings don't move. The tail curl and white left sock make a mirror image show.
- **Beat** (`beat.js`): 150 BPM, one turn per beat. While the default video plays, `tick` steers toward `songAngle(t)` the short way round, locking only the turn. `songClock` smooths `getCurrentTime`. Other videos, reduced motion and a missing player run free. `window.cat4d.sync()` is for tests.
- **Picture**: the YouTube player in a `CSS3DObject`, from `screenSlice` (affine, plus a `clip-path`) or `screenShadow` (projective `matrix3d`). The CSS layer sits above the canvas, so no 3D object draws over it; it hides when backfacing, outside the slab or nearly edge-on. Music keeps playing out of your slice. `?v=<id>` plays another video. YouTube refuses some networks, so the tests block it.
