4D Spinning Cat

The OIIA cat, round in w as well as x, y and z, spins in four dimensions in front of a 4D TV. You stand in a 3D slice of their world, as in the explorer: walk, step ana or kata, or tilt your slice, and you see whatever crosses it, the picture on the TV included.

Music

“OIIA OIIA (Spinning Cat)” by W&W, playing from YouTube on the 4D TV. The cat spins while it plays.
